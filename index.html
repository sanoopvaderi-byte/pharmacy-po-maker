Automatically backs up everything to your Google Drive: Supplier details, Product mapping, Order placed history, Sent order history, Staff lists, and Settings — saved as a full restore file, daily snapshots, and a readable Google Sheet.
One-time 1-minute setup (free, connects directly to your Google Drive)
  1. Open script.google.com (signed in to your Google Drive account) and click New project.
  2. Click 📋 Copy Apps Script below, delete any default text in Code.gs, and paste the copied code.
  3. Click Deploy → New deployment → gear icon → Web app. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone. Click Deploy and approve Google permissions.
  4. Copy the Web app URL (https://script.google.com/macros/s/…/exec), paste it below, and click Save & Connect Drive.
